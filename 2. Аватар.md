**E) Trigger words (resonance):**   
team, employees, dream team, while you sleep, voice, sounds like you, weekend, one hour a day, finally, system, operating system, freedom, breathe.

**F) Reject words (corporate/tech jargon):**   
workforce, deployment, leverage, synergy, harness, AI stack, integrations, no-code workflow, agents, LLMs, prompt engineering, vector database, n8n.

**G) Objections to neutralize on the sales page:**

* "I've bought courses before and didn't finish." → Counter: "Built in a weekend, runs forever. No 47-module slog."  
* "I'm not technical." → Counter: "If you can talk, you can build this. Voice from your phone."  
* "Will this work for MY business?" → Counter: Show 3 avatars side by side (coach, designer, beauty business).  
* "I tried ChatGPT, it was generic." → Counter: "This is why generic AI fails — and what Claude Projects \+ Skills \+ Connectors do differently."  
* Price: $97 reads as impulse for someone at $3–15K/mo. Anchor against the $97/mo Sintra is charging forever.

**H) Decision triggers (what actually closes the sale):**

* Specific time promises ("one weekend").  
* Specific daily promises ("1 hour a day from your phone").  
* Identity reframe ("from solopreneur to CEO of a one-person company").  
* Founder-pricing soft urgency.  
* 30-day no-questions money-back guarantee at $97.
