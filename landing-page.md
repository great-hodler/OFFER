**❌ NEVER USE:** workforce, deployment, leverage, synergy, AI stack, integrations, no-code workflow, LLMs, prompt engineering, vector database, n8n, automation pipeline, agentic, ecosystem

**✅ ALWAYS USE:** team, employees, dream team, while you sleep, voice, sounds like you, weekend, one hour a day, finally, system, freedom, from your phone

**Voice:** Conversational. Direct. Emma is talking to her best friend who's also a solopreneur — not pitching a corporate boardroom.

**Sentence rules:** Short. Rarely more than 2 clauses. One idea per sentence. Use "And" instead of "But" for softer transitions.

**Proof rules:** Always specific numbers. "15 hours" not "lots of time." "One weekend" not "quickly." "$18K/month" not "six figures."

**Emoji rules:** Use sparingly — only as visual punctuation in section headers, CTA areas, and proof bars. Never in body copy.
